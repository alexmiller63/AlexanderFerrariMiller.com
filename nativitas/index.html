A new project by Alexander Ferrari Miller

Nativitas

Your sky. Your moment. Your story.

What did the heavens look like when you were born? Nativitas is being developed to reconstruct the celestial positions at a particular place and moment in time, using astronomy as its foundation and exploring astrology as a historical and cultural tradition.

Ephemera

Calculate solar-system positions and local rise/set times for a date, time and location. Includes city search and a downloadable report.

Natal Chart

Generate a chart for your birth date, time and place, with Placidus houses, planet positions and major aspects.

Synastry

Compare two birth charts in a bi-wheel, with cross-chart aspects and house overlays.

Your Birth Sky

Explore the Sun, Moon, planets, and constellations at your birth date, time, and location.

Accurate Astronomy

Computed celestial positions, with transparent methods and clear distinctions between measured facts and interpretations.

Traditions & History

Learn how people, including historical astronomers, interpreted celestial configurations across centuries.

Accessible by Design

A useful free experience is the goal, with optional enhancements planned at a simple one-time price.

Ephemera is available now. Birth charts and other features remain under development. Astrology's claims about personality and future events have not been scientifically validated.
