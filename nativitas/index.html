Nativitas

Your sky. Your moment. Your story.

What did the heavens look like when you were born? Nativitas is being developed to reconstruct the celestial positions at a particular place and moment in time, using astronomy as its foundation and exploring astrology as a historical and cultural tradition.

Ephemera

Calculate solar-system positions and local rise/set times for a date, time and location. Includes city search and a downloadable report.

Natal

Explore your birth sky with a natal chart for your birth date, time and place, including planet positions, Placidus houses and major aspects.

Synastry

Compare two birth charts in a bi-wheel, with cross-chart aspects and house overlays.

About Nativitas

Discover the astronomy, historical traditions and accessible experience behind Nativitas.
