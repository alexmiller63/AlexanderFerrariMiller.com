## Jupiter–Saturn

### Jupiter–Saturn — Conjunction

The conjunction traditionally joins expansive ambitions with caution and restraint. Its central theme is the effort to give ideals a durable, practical form.

### Jupiter–Saturn — Sextile

The sextile suggests that optimism and prudence can cooperate. Careful planning may help turn broad intentions into sustained work.

### Jupiter–Saturn — Square

The square depicts friction between growth and limitation. Ambitious plans may require revision when resources or responsibilities impose constraints.

### Jupiter–Saturn — Trine

The trine emphasizes patient development. Long-range thinking and moderation may help the individual pursue substantial goals.

### Jupiter–Saturn — Opposition

The opposition portrays a pull between confidence and doubt. Judging when to expand and when to conserve becomes the central task.

## Uranus–Neptune

### Uranus–Neptune — Conjunction

The conjunction suggests an intersection of unconventional thought and imaginative ideals. Its slow movement makes it a broad generational configuration rather than a distinctive individual trait.

### Uranus–Neptune — Sextile

The sextile is traditionally read as an opportunity for inventive imagination. Because both planets move slowly, any personal significance requires broader chart context.

### Uranus–Neptune — Square

The square symbolizes friction between disruptive change and idealistic expectations. It describes a collective theme more readily than a personal characteristic.

### Uranus–Neptune — Trine

The trine suggests a possible harmony between innovation and imagination. Its long duration calls for caution in making individual predictions.

### Uranus–Neptune — Opposition

The opposition portrays tension between radical change and diffuse ideals. As a slow-moving planetary pattern, it should not be used alone to characterize a person.
