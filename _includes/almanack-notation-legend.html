Observing aid — these glyphs say how the target is intended to be observed; they do not identify the kind of astronomical object.

Naked eye Binoculars Telescope Substantial telescope

V followed by a number is visual magnitude; smaller or more negative numbers are brighter. If no observing-aid glyph is shown, the Almanack is not assigning an observing aid for that entry.

Sky state

Civil twilight — from sunset/sunrise to the Sun 6° below the horizon. Nautical twilight — Sun 6° to 12° below the horizon. Astronomical twilight — Sun 12° to 18° below the horizon. Night — Sun more than 18° below the horizon. Three stars above the horizon and the Sun below it represent the sky state at the selected observer time.

The twilight glyphs place the Sun progressively farther below the horizon; one, two, and three rays above the horizon distinguish civil, nautical, and astronomical twilight.
