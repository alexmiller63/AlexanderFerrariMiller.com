What the report contains

Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Ceres, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune and Pluto. Earth is the observer's origin, not an output row. The HTML and JSON reports include right ascension, declination, ecliptic coordinates, altitude, azimuth, and rise/set events.

Method and limitations

Positions are recalculated as time advances; rise/set times are not estimated from the weekly 9 PM snapshot. Coordinates use topocentric apparent positions; RA/Dec use ICRF axes. Values are provisional pending validation. The model uses standard atmospheric refraction and the Sun’s and Moon’s apparent radii, without local terrain, horizon dip or weather corrections, and the time scan may miss grazing events.

Calculations run on your device. City search and astronomy files come from this site; your chosen coordinates are not sent to a calculation service. The browser may cache downloaded files for later visits.
