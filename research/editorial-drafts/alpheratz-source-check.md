# Alpheratz — independent source check (editorial draft)

## Verified against primary research

1. Kochukhov, Adelman, Gulliver & Piskunov, *Nature Physics* **3**, 526–529 (2007), DOI 10.1038/nphys648, explicitly report **seven years** of monitoring of mercury spots in **non-magnetic** α Andromedae.
2. The authors use an **indirect stellar-surface mapping technique** and show that the inferred mercury-rich structures **evolved**. Thus the draft's description of changing atmospheric mercury abundance patterns is supported.
3. The authors present atomic diffusion and non-equilibrium dynamics as a **plausible explanation**, not a conclusively established mechanism. The draft should retain cautious language.
4. The publication's figures identify the Hg II **398.4 nm** spectral line and maps from **1998, 2002, 2004**. These are useful optional details if the final story needs a concrete example.

Primary source: https://www.nature.com/articles/nphys648

## Independent observing cross-check

Phil Harrington, “The Great Square,” *Cloudy Nights* (2023), identifies Alpheratz (α Andromedae), Scheat, Markab and Algenib as the four corners and confirms that Alpheratz is formally assigned to Andromeda rather than Pegasus.

Source: https://www.cloudynights.com/articles/column/phil-harrington-s/cosmic-challenge-the-great-square-r3401/
