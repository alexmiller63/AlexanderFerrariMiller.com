## Fast-path publication policy proposed

1. Correct or remove the **specific** unsupported or erroneous claims listed above; preserve good original prose.
2. Treat Finder-artwork verification as an independent workstream, not a reason to stop editing prose.
3. Verify numerical and stellar-classification claims against suitable catalogs or literature, not only the generated draft's citation list.
4. Produce all ten corrected manuscripts on an editorial branch, then review them as a batch. No paid calls needed to re-generate text.
5. Only mark individual manuscripts as approved when their facts, links and final copy have actually been checked. Do not equate an automated screening pass with publication approval.

## Immediate conclusion

The pilot drafts are **not** all publication-ready as written. Caph, Algenib and Gamma Cassiopeiae contain material scientific problems. The initial Segin concern was corrected after consulting SIMBAD directly; the Be classification is supported. This explains why a quick, **targeted** correction pass is worthwhile, but does **not** justify spending an hour on each story or blocking all writing on missing Finder SVGs.
