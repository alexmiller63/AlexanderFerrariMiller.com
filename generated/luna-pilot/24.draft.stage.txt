# Chamukuy, a Small Pulse in Taurus

*In the Hyades, a modest naked-eye star offers a close-up lesson in stellar motion and change.*

On a clear winter evening, Taurus rises with a familiar shape: a V of stars, with orange Aldebaran marking one side and the Hyades cluster filling the other. Near the V’s bend is a close pair. One of those stars is Chamukuy
