# Merope: A Blue Flame in the Pleiades

*Its catalog entries offer a precise starting point for finding the blue-white star—and a reminder that a star’s brightness, color and distance are different kinds of information.*

Merope is one of the Pleiades’ bright stars, also cataloged as 23 Tauri and HIP 17608. Its familiar name may be the one skywatchers use, but the catalog labels help pin down exactly which object is meant. SIMBAD lists its visual magnitude as 4.18 and its spectral classification as B6IV(e).
