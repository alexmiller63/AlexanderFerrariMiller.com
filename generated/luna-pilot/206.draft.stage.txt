# Arp 225: A Quiet Galaxy with an Active Heart

*NGC 2655 is a broad, faint target for northern observers—and a reminder that a galaxy’s calm appearance can hide a busy center.*

The number Arp 225 sounds like a challenge: a far-off object filed among galaxies that caught astronomers’ attention. But through an eyepiece, NGC 2655 is less spectacle than subtlety. Its light spreads across several arcminutes, a soft patch against the dark. The reward is not a dramatic silhouette. It is the knowledge that this muted glow belongs to a galaxy whose center is classified as active.

NGC 2655 is listed as a Seyfert 2 galaxy, a kind of galaxy with an active nucleus. Its recorded redshift is about 0.00485, and its optical classification is E-S0, a label spanning elliptical to lenticular forms.
