# MCG 8-2-10: A Small Galaxy with a Double Identity

*In Cassiopeia, a faint companion of NGC 147 offers a lesson in how galaxies can be classified—and how patient observing brings a diffuse target into view.*

Some galaxies announce themselves with bright arms and brilliant cores. MCG 8-2-10 is subtler. Also known as NGC 185, it occupies a patch of sky in Cassiopeia where a telescope may show not a crisp cosmic jewel, but a soft, extended glow. Its quiet appearance is part of the appeal: this is a galaxy to seek out slowly, letting the eye adjust and the faint light accumulate.

Catalog data give NGC 185 an apparent visual magnitude of about 9.2 and an optical size of roughly 11.5 by 9.6 arcminutes.
