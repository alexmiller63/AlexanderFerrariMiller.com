# Aspidiske: A Quiet Beacon in the Southern Sea

*An easy naked-eye star in Carina, Iota Carinae is a distant supergiant whose steady light rewards a closer look.*

On a clear southern evening, Aspidiske does not need an introduction from a telescope. It shines plainly to the unaided eye, a pale point in the constellation Carina. Yet its light has crossed a remarkable distance before reaching us: measurements listed by SIMBAD put it about 770 light-years away. The same catalogue identifies it as Iota Carinae and gives its position as right ascension 9h 17m, declination −59° 16′ (J2000), a useful address for finding it on a sky chart. [SIMBAD: Iota Carinae](https://simbad.cds.unistra.fr/simbad/sim-id?Ident=HIP%2045556)
