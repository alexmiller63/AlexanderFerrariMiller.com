# Reading Beta Ursae Minoris

*One star’s catalog entry turns a point of light into a set of measurable quantities: position, motion, distance and brightness.*

A star can be familiar in the sky and still be described most precisely by a catalog number. SIMBAD’s entry for HIP 72607 identifies it as “bet UMi,” or Beta Ursae Minoris, and provides a compact portrait in measurements. The record gives coordinates, motion, parallax, a spectral classification and brightness values
