# Star Notes

## 2026-09-07 — Star Notes tell us how much

The Star Note's primary job is to answer: **How much is there to see, notice, understand, and connect?**

A star may qualify for the calendar through several independent reasons. Those reasons should not create duplicate calendar entries for the same physical observing target; instead, they enrich the Star Note.

Useful Star Note material includes:

- α or β status within a constellation.
- Bright-star significance and naked-eye prominence.
- Membership in one or more asterisms.
- The other stars that complete those asterisms and how the pattern helps orient the observer.
- Color, multiplicity, variability, notable maxima or minima, and exceptional brightness events.
- Historical, scientific, navigational, and cultural significance when observationally useful.
- Practical observing guidance and what the star helps the observer find next.

Betelgeuse is a model case: one calendar target can simultaneously carry α Orionis status, exceptional naked-eye prominence, constellation-figure significance, asterism relationships such as the Winter Triangle, strong color, variability, and the 2019–2020 Great Dimming. Those relationships belong in one rich Star Note rather than several redundant calendar entries.

The editorial division of labor is therefore:

**Calendar: when. Star Note: how much.**
