Mirzam: The Bright Star of Canis Major

An illuminating guide to Mirzam, a luminous star in the constellation Canis Major.

Mirzam, also known as Beta Canis Majoris, is a prominent and luminous star residing in the constellation Canis Major, one of the most recognizable regions of the night sky. Positioned at approximately right ascension 6.3783 hours and declination -17.9559 degrees, Mirzam gleams as a bright star that has captured the attention of stargazers for centuries.
