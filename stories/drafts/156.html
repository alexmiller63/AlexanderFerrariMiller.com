Arp 135: A Peculiar Galaxy in Perseus

Exploring the distinctive features of this intriguing galaxy and its place in the cosmos.

Classified as a galaxy within the vast universe, it is part of the Arp catalog, which highlights peculiar and anomalous galaxies showcasing unique structural or evolutionary features. Though detailed distance measurements are not definitively available, Arp 135 is an example of the myriad extragalactic bodies that astronomers study to understand galactic formation and interaction.
