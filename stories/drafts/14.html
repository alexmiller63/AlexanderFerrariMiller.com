17 Tauri: Reading a Star in the Catalog

HIP 17499’s measurements offer a compact introduction to stellar classification, distance and motion.

A star can be familiar by one name and precise by another. The catalogue identifies the object discussed here as 17 Tauri, or HIP 17499, and classifies it as a Be star. Its listed spectral type is B6IIIe. Those catalog labels do more than identify a point of light: they summarize how astronomers classify the star and record its properties.

The spectral type can be read in parts. “B6” is the star’s spectral class and subtype; “III” is its luminosity class; and the final “e” denotes emission features in its spectrum.
