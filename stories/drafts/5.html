Cih: A Stellar Landmark in the W-shaped Cassiopeia

An enduring point of brightness in the W-shaped constellation, offering navigational and observational insights.

Cih is a notable star within the prominent asterism often identified as Cassiopeia's W — a distinctive and easily recognizable pattern in the northern sky. Situated at roughly 0.9451 hours of right ascension and a declination of approximately 60.7167 degrees, this star contributes to the striking outline that characterizes Cassiopeia, one of the most famous constellations in the celestial sphere. As a potential physical object candidate, Cih stands out due to its relative brightness and position, making it a useful reference point for both amateur and professional astronomers.

The star is cataloged under various identifiers, including HIP 4427, and is recognized as a figure star within star atlas traditions and modern catalogues such as Hipparcos. Its coordinates align with the accepted positions for figure stars used in celestial navigation and star mapping, emphasizing its role as a stable and reliable point of reference. Although its precise spectral characteristics are not detailed here, its inclusion in brightness catalogs indicates it is part of the brighter stars cataloged at magnitudes brighter than 2, making it an accessible target for observation.

In the broader context of celestial navigation and observation, stars like Cih serve as essential markers for understanding stellar distributions and for orienting oneself in the night sky. Their stable positions and brightness lend them importance beyond their aesthetic appeal, providing fixed points against which the movements of other celestial objects can be gauged. Whether used for educational purposes, star mapping, or navigation, stars in constellations like Cassiopeia continue to inspire and assist skywatchers around the world.

As a figure star, Cih not only enriches our appreciation of the night sky but also underscores the interconnectedness of star cataloging, navigation, and cultural astronomy. Its consistency and position make Cih a fixture in the celestial tapestry, an ever-present reminder of the universe’s enduring complexity and beauty.
