Larawag: A Nearby K-Type Giant

A star can look like a single point of light while carrying a surprisingly detailed record of its position, motion and physical classification. The catalogue identifies HIP 82396 as Epsilon Scorpii, or “eps Sco,” and records it as a high-proper-motion star. Its catalogue measurements place it among the Sun’s relatively close stellar neighbors: its parallax implies a distance of about 64 light-years.

The parallax is 51.19 milliarcseconds, with a reported uncertainty of 0.22 milliarcseconds. Parallax is the apparent change in a star’s position against more distant background objects as Earth travels around the Sun.
