Alpha Pyx: A Celestial Beacon in the Pyx Constellation

An overview of the star known as Alpha Pyx, its position, and significance in the night sky.

Alpha Pyx, designated by Bayer as Alp Pyx, is a prominent star situated within the constellation Pyx. Its celestial coordinates place it at approximately 8.7265 hours of right ascension and about -33.1864 degrees of declination, guiding observers to its location in the southern celestial hemisphere. As a figure star recognized in star almanacks and celestial catalogs, Alpha Pyx holds a noteworthy position among the stars that adorn our night sky.

This star is classified as a physical object candidate, indicating its role as a natural astronomical feature worthy of observation and study. While specific details such as its spectral type and luminosity are not included in this overview, stars of its category typically vary in brightness and spectral characteristics, contributing to the richness of the celestial tapestry.
