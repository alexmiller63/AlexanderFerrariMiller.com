NGC 362: A Southern Glow in the Dark

*Its catalog coordinates place this globular cluster deep in the southern sky, where a small patch of light carries a wealth of information.*

NGC 362 is easy to reduce to a few lines in a catalog: a type, a brightness, a position, an apparent size. But each number describes a different part of the view. SIMBAD classifies NGC 362 as a globular cluster and lists its visual magnitude as 6.58 and its angular size as 12.9 arcminutes. Those entries offer a starting point—not a complete portrait of what an observer will see. [SIMBAD: NGC 362](https://simbad.cds.unistra.fr/simbad/sim-id?Ident=NGC%20362)
