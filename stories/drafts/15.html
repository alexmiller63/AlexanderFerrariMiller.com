Taygeta, a Blue Note in the Pleiades

*Take a chart or astronomy app to this crowded star field, then pause over Taygeta: one point of light with a more complicated identity than the eye can reveal.*

Taygeta rewards a little patience. In a cluster patterned with close-set stars, it can be easy to sweep past the one you came to find. Start by locating the Pleiades, then use a chart or app that displays individual star names. The brightest points make a useful framework, but Taygeta may not stand out immediately. Under a dark sky, try to identify it without optical aid; binoculars can help separate the cluster’s many points into a more legible scene.
