Pleiades

Pleiades is an observing target in Taurus carried by the weekly Calendar.

Why it is here: The Pleiades are one of the sky’s best-known naked-eye open clusters. People have watched and named this compact star group since antiquity, and its seasonal return has long made it a natural marker in the yearly sky.

How to find it: Find Orion’s three Belt stars, follow their line toward orange Aldebaran and the Hyades / V of Taurus, then continue in the same general direction to the compact Pleiades cluster.
