Cursa

Cursa is a stellar reference in Eri used by the weekly observing calendar.

Why it is here: The Calendar selected Cursa for this week’s fixed-sky observing sequence; its permanent object identity ties the weekly entry to the same star used by the finder.

How to find it: Establish the preserved Eri constellation figure from its labeled reference stars, then use the target’s plotted position rather than searching for an isolated star.
