Spica

Spica is a fixed-sky stellar reference in Vir used by the weekly observing calendar.

Why it is here: The Calendar selected Spica for this week’s fixed-sky observing sequence; its permanent object identity ties the weekly entry to the same star used by the finder.

How to find it: Trace the curve of the Big Dipper's handle to Arcturus, then continue the same sweep to Spica.
