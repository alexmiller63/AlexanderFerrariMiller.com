Kilroy sightings

Normandy

Kilroy became part of the visual folklore of the American advance across Europe.

From the Beachhead Inland

National Park Service accounts describe “Kilroy Was Here” as appearing from the beaches of Normandy onward. The phrase and peering figure traveled with servicemen as they moved through Europe, helping create the impression that Kilroy had somehow arrived everywhere first.

Documented tradition · World War IINational Park Service interpretation identifies Normandy among the places associated with the wartime spread of Kilroy.
