Angels Landing Recollection

An oral-history conversation from Zion Canyon preserves a memory of seeing “Kilroy was here” on the route to Angels Landing.

A Remembered Sighting

In a 2004 Pioneer Voices of Zion Canyon oral-history interview, Janis Kali asks Betsy Alford whether she remembers going up Angels Landing and seeing “Kilroy was here.” Alford replies that she remembers seeing those markings, while contrasting them with her own reluctance to carve her name into rock.

Oral history · 2004 recollectionThis is evidence of a remembered Kilroy sighting, not a surviving inscription verified at the site today.
