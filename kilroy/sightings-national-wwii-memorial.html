National World War II Memorial

At the National World War II Memorial in Washington, D.C., Kilroy has become part of the official landscape of remembrance.

Two Hidden Kilroys

The National World War II Memorial contains two deliberately hidden Kilroy engravings. One is on the Atlantic side and the other on the Pacific side, turning the once-illicit servicemen’s mark into an official part of national remembrance.

Documented · Present dayThe National Park Service identifies two Kilroy engravings at the memorial, one associated with each theater.
