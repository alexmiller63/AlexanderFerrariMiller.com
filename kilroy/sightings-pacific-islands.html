Pacific Islands

Across the Pacific, Kilroy became a servicemen’s signature of arrival — and of somebody having arrived just before you.

Across the Pacific

National Park Service interpretation describes Kilroy appearing from European battlefields to the jungles of the Pacific. The mark moved with American forces through bases, ships, equipment, and newly reached territory.

Documented tradition · World War IIThe National Park Service includes the Pacific theater in its account of Kilroy’s extraordinary wartime reach.
