The Stalin Story

One of the great Kilroy legends claims that Joseph Stalin encountered “Kilroy was here” in a VIP bathroom at the Potsdam Conference and demanded to know who Kilroy was.

Legend · 1945The story is irresistible folklore, but it should remain labeled as legend unless stronger contemporary evidence emerges.
