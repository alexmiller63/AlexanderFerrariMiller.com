Kilroy sightings

Rosie the Riveter Visitor Center

At the Rosie the Riveter/World War II Home Front National Historical Park, Kilroy is still hiding in plain sight.

Find Kilroy

The National Park Service keeps the Kilroy tradition alive at its visitor center in Richmond, California. A Kilroy drawing is hidden somewhere inside, and the park explicitly invites visitors to find him.

Documented · Present dayThe National Park Service photographs the visitor-center Kilroy and challenges visitors to spot him.
