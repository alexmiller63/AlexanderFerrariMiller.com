Kilroy sightings

Fore River Shipyard

One leading origin story places the words “Kilroy was here” inside ships under construction at the Fore River Shipyard in Quincy, Massachusetts.

James J. Kilroy’s Inspection Mark

James J. Kilroy worked as a checker at the Fore River Shipyard during World War II. Accounts preserved by the U.S. Naval Institute describe him marking inspected work with the words “Kilroy was here,” making the shipyard itself a foundational location in the Kilroy story.
