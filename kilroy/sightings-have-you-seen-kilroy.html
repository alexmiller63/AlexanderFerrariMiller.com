Kilroy sightings

Have You Seen Kilroy?

Kilroy’s trail did not end with World War II. The catalog remains open to sightings that can be located, dated, and checked.

The Trail Continues

A useful sighting includes a location, approximate date, what was seen, and — whenever possible — a photograph, archival record, publication, or firsthand account that lets the story be checked.

Open catalogDocumented examples, contemporary reports, later recollections, and legends can all belong here as long as their evidentiary status remains clear.
