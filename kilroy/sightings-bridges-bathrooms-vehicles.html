Kilroy sightings

Bridges, Bathrooms, and Vehicles

Kilroy’s fame came not from one monument but from appearing almost anywhere servicemen could leave a mark.

Everywhere There Was a Surface

National Park Service history describes wartime Kilroy graffiti on ship holds, bathrooms, bridges, and military equipment. The variety matters: Kilroy was portable folklore, repeatedly recreated rather than confined to a single surviving inscription.

Documented wartime traditionThe National Park Service identifies these ordinary military and transit spaces as characteristic places where Kilroy appeared.
