Where Kilroy turned up

Kilroy Sightings

Kilroy’s defining trick was getting there first. During World War II the phrase and peering figure traveled with American servicemen across ships, bases, vehicles, bridges, bathrooms, battlefields, and both major theaters of war. Later generations kept finding — and making — Kilroys of their own.

Not every famous Kilroy story has the same evidentiary weight. This catalog separates documented examples from contemporary reports, later recollections, and legends.

LEGEND · 1945

Potsdam Conference

One of the great Kilroy stories claims that Joseph Stalin encountered “Kilroy was here” in a VIP bathroom at Potsdam and demanded to know who Kilroy was. It is irresistible folklore — and should be treated as folklore unless stronger contemporary evidence emerges.

Have You Seen Kilroy?

This catalog is intentionally open-ended. A useful sighting includes a location, approximate date, what was seen, and — whenever possible — a photograph, archival record, publication, or firsthand account that lets the story be checked.
