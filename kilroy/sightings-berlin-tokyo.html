Kilroy sightings

Berlin and Tokyo

By the end of the war, Kilroy’s imagined itinerary stretched toward the symbolic endpoints of the Allied campaigns.

The Long Haul

The U.S. Naval Institute’s history summarizes Kilroy’s extraordinary spread by saying that before the war ended the character had appeared along the long routes toward Berlin and Tokyo. The wording captures what made the graffiti memorable: Kilroy was less a single location than a recurring companion to servicemen moving across the world.

Historical accountThis chapter treats Berlin and Tokyo as the endpoints in the documented account of Kilroy’s wartime spread, not as claims about a particular surviving inscription at either location.
