Natural Calendar

How many numbered calendar days does each astronomical zodiac month contain?

Aries 1 is the local civil date on which the Sun reaches 0° tropical ecliptic longitude. Taurus 1 begins at 30°, and so on. Each zodiac month includes its first date and ends the day before the next ingress date. The year runs from Aries 1 to the next Aries 1. Dates are assigned in America/Los_Angeles, including daylight saving time. A natural leap year has 366 numbered calendar days. Astronomical ingress times are approximated from a standard solar-longitude series; consult the Almanack ephemeris for precision near midnight.
