Star Almanack

Eclipses

The eclipse engine is now accurate enough to become part of the Almanack while validation and precision work continue separately.

What the Almanack will show

Each published eclipse entry can present the date, eclipse type, greatest-eclipse time in UTC, and concise observing or visibility information.

Publication and research remain separate

The Almanack can publish useful eclipse predictions without declaring the underlying numerical work finished. Improvements to ephemerides, time scales, geometry, and validation can continue behind the published interface.

Next

The next implementation step is to connect this page to generated eclipse data from the Star Almanack calculation repository.
