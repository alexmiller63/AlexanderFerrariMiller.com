Calculation conventions

Geographic latitude north and longitude east are positive. Use a UTC date/time such as 2026-10-22T21:00:00Z. The positions correspond to that instant; the rise/set events are computed for its UTC calendar date, and may fall on a different local calendar date.

Included: Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Ceres, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune and Pluto. Earth supplies the observing origin and is excluded from the output.

The engine recomputes the apparent altitude at successive times and bisects each detected horizon crossing. It does not use the weekly 9 PM snapshot to estimate rise and set.

Current limitations: 1900–2100 kernel range; standard-horizon approximations; no terrain or local atmospheric correction; near-grazing events require further validation. A direct in-browser form and city selector are planned but are not available yet.
