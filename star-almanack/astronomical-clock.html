—Approximate calendar date when this right ascension culminates at 9:00 p.m. local apparent solar time (LAT). Neighboring dates correspond to neighboring stars along the meridian.

Solar and sidereal results are calculated from UTC and longitude, not from your device's displayed clock hour. Apparent solar time uses an approximate equation of time. The meridian calendar uses the 9 p.m. local apparent time culmination convention (approximately Sun RA + 9 hours).
