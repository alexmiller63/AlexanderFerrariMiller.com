Meridian calendar

—Approximate calendar date when the Sun occupies this right ascension. Stars at this RA culminate around local solar noon on that date; their midnight culmination occurs roughly six months away. Adjacent dates correspond to neighboring right ascensions.

Solar and sidereal results are calculated from UTC and longitude, not from your device's displayed clock hour. Apparent solar time uses an approximate equation of time. The meridian calendar is a seasonal coordinate, not a claim that stars are visible in daylight.
