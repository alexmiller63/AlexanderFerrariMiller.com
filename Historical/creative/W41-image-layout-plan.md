# W41 Image Layout Plan

W41 is the birthday week. The page already contains the two ephemeris tables and Star Notes.

## Placement

The three canonical Planet Finder images go together directly beneath the two ephemeris tables, in this order:

1. Greek / Symbols
2. Latin
3. Mixed / Learner

The NFM-15 finder and Aquarius finder are embedded within the relevant Star Notes, so each image visually supports the astronomical text rather than appearing as a separate gallery.

## Design rule

Blend the images into the existing W41 typography, spacing, and content flow. Do not alter the existing ephemerides or Star Notes text merely to accommodate the images. Preserve the three frozen Planet Finder templates as distinct notation modes.
