# Calendar Rules

## 2026-09-07 — Calendar tells us when

The calendar's primary job is to answer: **When should I look?**

Calendar membership is deliberate rather than a dump of every catalog relationship. However, asterism membership is itself a valid reason for a star to qualify for calendar inclusion because learning the member stars helps an observer learn the sky pattern.

When the same physical observing target qualifies through multiple layers — for example α/β status, bright-star status, asterism membership, or another editorial layer — print one calendar entry for that target rather than multiple redundant entries. Preserve all qualifying relationships in source data and notes.

The asterism itself receives its own independently calculated center-visibility day from its zero-margin spherical boundary centroid.

For constellations, α visibility, β visibility, and geometric-center visibility are separate calculated calendar events.
