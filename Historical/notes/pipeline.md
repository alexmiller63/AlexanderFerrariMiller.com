# Pipeline

## 2026-09-07 — Multi-year architecture

2026 is the reference implementation, not a permanent special case. Shared calendar, visibility, ephemeris, constellation, asterism, and presentation rules should be year-independent. Year-specific astronomical results are inputs to the shared pipeline.

Prefer a single generator parameterized by year over separate hand-maintained generators for 2025, 2026, 2027, and later editions.
