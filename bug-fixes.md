# Star Almanack Bug Fix Log

Persistent record of discovered bugs, regressions, and their disposition.

## Rules

- Append new bug entries; do not silently remove prior entries.
- Record the symptom, affected component, and disposition when known.
- Mark resolved items as resolved rather than deleting them.
- Architectural discussions and design decisions belong in `notes.md`.
- Fix defects at their generator or source whenever possible; do not patch generated weekly output.
