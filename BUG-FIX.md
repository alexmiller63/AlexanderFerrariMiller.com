# Star Almanack Bug/Fix Log

Ongoing record of defects, their state, fixes, and resolutions.

Use this file for bugs and corrective work. Architectural discussions and design decisions remain in `notes.md`.

## States

- **Open** — confirmed defect; not yet fixed.
- **In progress** — diagnosis or corrective work is underway.
- **Fixed — verification pending** — a fix has been committed but the affected generator/workflow/output has not yet been verified.
- **Resolved** — the fix has been verified in the intended output or workflow.
- **Deferred** — confirmed issue intentionally postponed.

## Entry format

### YYYY-MM-DD — Short bug title

**State:** Open

**Bug:** What is wrong and where it appears.

**Cause:** Root cause, when known.

**Fix:** What was changed.

**Resolution:** How the correction was verified, including the relevant commit or workflow when useful.

---

Append new entries. Do not erase resolved bugs; update their state and resolution so the history remains useful.
